Store Safety Rules

Food Handling
Employees must wash hands and wear gloves before handling any open food items. Gloves must be changed between raw and ready-to-eat food handling.

Fire and Emergency Procedures
In the event of a fire alarm, employees must guide customers to the nearest marked exit and assemble at the designated parking-lot meeting point. Do not use elevators.

Equipment Safety
The box compactor must only be operated by employees who have completed compactor safety training. Untrained employees must not operate it under any circumstances.
